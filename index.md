# Astute Stay Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 18 November 2026**

Astute Stay is a property and paying-guest accommodation management app. This policy explains how Astute Stay handles information when property owners, organization administrators, and their staff use the app. The organization that operates a property decides what guest information it enters and is responsible for providing any notices required for that information.

## Information handled by Astute Stay

Depending on the features an organization uses, Astute Stay may store and process:

- Account details, such as a staff member's name, email address, role, and organization membership.
- Guest details entered by the organization, such as name, phone number, email address, check-in and checkout dates, room and bed assignment, rent, and status.
- Property information, such as organization and building names, addresses, contact details, floors, rooms, and beds.
- Billing and operational records, such as invoices, due dates, rent changes, payment amounts, payment dates and methods, discounts, advances, expenses, and expense categories.
- Information included in files that an administrator chooses to import, export, download, or share.
- Basic local app preferences, such as selected dashboard filters and collapsed sections, stored on the device.

Astute Stay does not collect card numbers or bank login credentials for payment processing. Payment entries record payments that an organization says it has received; the app does not itself transfer money.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate staff, provide the selected organization's property-management features, maintain guest and billing records, calculate balances and reports, support imports and exports, enforce role-based access, and troubleshoot service errors. Information is not used for third-party advertising.

## Where information is stored and shared

The app sends account and organization data to its backend service, Supabase, for authentication and storage. Data is restricted by organization membership and access permissions. The organization’s authorized administrators and staff can access information according to their assigned permissions.

When a user exports or shares a PDF or spreadsheet, the file is provided to the device’s download or share feature. The user chooses whether and with whom to share it. Astute Stay does not send those exports to recipients automatically.

Astute Stay does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed if required by law or to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

## Security

Astute Stay uses authenticated access and organization-scoped permissions. Connections to the Supabase service use HTTPS. Users should keep their sign-in credentials private and contact their organization administrator if access should be removed. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

Guest, property, billing, and expense records are retained for the organization that created them until that organization removes them or requests their removal, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations and backup retention. Staff access can be disabled or removed by an organization administrator. Guests should contact the property or organization that entered their information to request correction or deletion. Organization administrators can contact the app developer through the repository's [GitHub issue page](https://github.com/yesuvadian/astute-stay-privacy-policy/issues); do not post personal, guest, or account information publicly.

Some financial or audit records may need to remain available to the organization for operational or legal record-keeping, even after a guest's stay ends.

## Children

Astute Stay is a business tool for property operators and is not designed for children to use directly. Organizations should not enter information about children unless they have an appropriate legal basis and required permissions.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is published.

## Contact

For privacy questions, organization administrators can contact the app developer through the [Astute Stay GitHub issue page](https://github.com/yesuvadian/astute-stay-privacy-policy/issues). Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
